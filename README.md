# People Analytics: Workforce & Capability Insights

This Power BI project analyzes workforce composition, employee development indicators, and attrition patterns to uncover key organizational insights and potential workforce risks.

The analysis combines traditional HR metrics such as headcount, compensation, job satisfaction, work-life balance, training participation, overtime, and employee attrition with an additional skill-focused perspective. To extend the analytical value of the original dataset, a custom skill framework was developed, assigning employees to core skill areas, future-critical capabilities, and workforce risk categories.

# The dashboard explores questions such as:

- Which departments and job roles are most affected by attrition?
- Which workforce characteristics are associated with higher turnover risk?
- How is the organization structured across departments and functions?
- Which skill areas may require future investment and development?
- Where do potential workforce and skill risks emerge?

The project demonstrates data preparation in Power Query, DAX-based KPI development, exploratory workforce analytics, dashboard design, and the integration of custom business logic to support strategic HR decision-making.
